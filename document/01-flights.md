# ABC Company travel policy: flights (sample data)

## Class of travel
All domestic flights are booked in Economy. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours, with approval from a Vice President.

## When to book
Book flights at least 7 days before travel where possible. Bookings made less than 48 hours before departure need a short note from the traveller's manager explaining why.

## Approval for extra cost
Any change that adds more than ₹3,000 per traveller (change fee plus fare difference) needs the manager's approval before the travel desk confirms it. Changes up to ₹3,000 per traveller can be made directly.

## Changes close to departure
Date changes within 24 hours of departure are handled by the travel desk, not the assistant, because seat availability and fees change quickly.
