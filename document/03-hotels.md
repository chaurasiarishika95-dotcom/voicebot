# ABC Company travel policy: hotels (sample data)

## Approved hotels and nightly limits
Book company-approved hotels. The nightly limit including taxes is ₹7,000 in Bangalore, ₹8,000 in Delhi, ₹8,500 in Bombay and ₹6,500 in Hyderabad. Stays above the limit need the manager's approval.

## Hotel check-in and check-out times
At the hotel, standard check-in is 2 pm and check-out is 12 noon. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability, and the hotel may charge for half a day.

## Hotel changes and cancellations
Hotel stays can usually be cancelled free up to 24 hours before check-in. After that, the hotel may charge one night. If a flight date changes, the linked hotel stay should be moved too.
