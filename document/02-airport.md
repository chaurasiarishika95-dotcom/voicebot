# Airport and airline rules (sample data)

## Baggage allowance (luggage)
On domestic Economy fares, each traveller can carry one cabin bag up to 7 kg and check in one bag (checked luggage) up to 15 kg. Extra baggage is paid by the company only if the trip needs it, for example equipment for a client visit, and the manager approves it before travel.

## Web check-in
Web check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before departure. Airport check-in counters close 45 minutes before departure. Travellers should reach the airport at least 2 hours before a domestic flight.

## ID needed at the airport
Carry a valid government photo ID, such as Aadhaar, passport, driving licence or voter ID. The name on the ID must match the name on the ticket.

## Name corrections
Names on tickets cannot be changed to a different person. A small spelling correction of up to 3 letters can be requested through the travel desk. Anything larger means cancelling and booking again, and the cancellation fee applies.
