# Refunds, expenses and insurance (sample data)

## Refund timelines
Refunds for cancelled flights and hotels go back to the company card used for the booking, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Non-refundable fares may return only taxes and fees.

## Meals and daily allowance
Travellers get a daily allowance of ₹1,500 for meals on domestic trips. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Local travel
Airport and local cabs booked through the company cab app are paid directly. Other local travel can be claimed with receipts.

## Travel insurance
All employees are covered by the company's group travel insurance for work trips. Report any incident to the travel desk within 48 hours.
