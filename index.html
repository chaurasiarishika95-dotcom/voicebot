This is a rule-based simulation, not a production NLU model, so unusual phrasing may not be recognized. The design point it's making: reschedules and cancellations are routine and fully automatable once the bot has backend access — the bot handles those completely on its own. Policy exceptions (like the business-class example) genuinely need a human judgment call, so it escalates those — quickly, and by name, not as a catch-all fallback.
