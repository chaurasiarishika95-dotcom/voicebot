How to use

What it is

A prototype voice assistant for corporate travellers after they've booked. It looks up flights and hotel stays, cancels after a confirmation, changes dates with the real fee and fare difference, checks the company's travel policy, and hands over to the travel desk with a ticket when it shouldn't act alone. All bookings, fares and policies are sample data for "ABC Company".

Steps

  1. Choose who's calling. Aarav (Employee) sees only his own bookings. Priya (Central Booker) can manage team and company bookings.
  2. Pick English or Hinglish. In Hinglish the bot listens for Hindi and Hinglish and replies in Hinglish.
  3. Click Start call, then press 🎙 Speak or type. Turn on Hands-free to have it listen again by itself after each reply.
  4. Press Speak while the bot is talking to interrupt it.

The agent console

The right-hand side is what a travel desk agent would see, not the caller. Frustration score adds points for each cause (repeating, wrong picks, angry words, fee disputes, long calls) and takes points off when a step succeeds. At 50 the bot offers a person; at 75 it hands over. It reads words, not tone of voice. Handoff ticket is what the agent receives, so the caller never has to repeat themselves.

How success is measured

  • Safe autonomous resolution rate: share of all calls the bot fully resolves with no critical error, no repeat call within 7 days and no reversal.
  • Always reported with: critical errors (target 0), missed handoffs (under 2%), and repeat contacts.
  • Test results so far (scripted test calls on sample data, not real traffic): 153 scripted calls with no wrong cancellations and no access errors. Handoffs fell from 65 to 45, because date changes and most fee disputes no longer need a person. 27 more tests cover the new flows.

What a real launch would need

  • Phone line: telephony (Exotel or Twilio) with streaming speech for low delay, interruptions, noisy lines and Indian accents.
  • Booking systems: a GDS or the booking tool (Amadeus, Sabre or the TMC's system). Quotes are estimates; the bot re-checks the price right before acting and asks again if it changed.
  • Multi-city trips: before cancelling or changing, the bot reads back the exact flight or hotel and gets a clear yes.
  • Policy and approvals: the company's real travel policy and approval workflow in place of the sample rules.
  • Audit: every action logged with what the caller was told, for disputes and liability.
Nothing here touches real bookings. Fares, fees, flight options and the re-check price changes are simulated to show how the bot behaves.
